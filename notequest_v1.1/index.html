Arena Mode

Arena Mode turns a mind map into a quiz. Create a mind map first.

Add more topics or custom questions in Mind Maps

Mode

Players share one keyboard and take turns. Player 1 answers with A S D F, Player 2 with J K L ;. You can also click or tap the answers.

How scoring works

  • Each correct answer earns 100 points.
  • Answer faster to earn up to 50 bonus points.
  • Hard difficulty multiplies points by 1.5.
  • Questions come only from your mind map topics, their descriptions and your own question-and-answer pairs.
